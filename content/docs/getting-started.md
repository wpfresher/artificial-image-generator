# Getting Started

This guide takes you from a fresh install to a featured image on every new post. If you haven't installed the plugin yet, start with the [Installation page](https://beautifulplugins.com/docs/image-generator/installation/).

## Step 1 — Create your first template

1. Go to **Image Generator → Image Templates → Add New**. The **Template Studio** opens with a choice of ready-made designs.
2. Pick one — *Bold headline* or *Classic centered* are good first choices — or start from a blank canvas.
3. Make it yours: click a layer on the canvas or in the **Layers** panel and change its colors, font or position in the panel on the right. Add your logo with an **Image** layer set to the site logo.
4. Use **Preview with** to check how it looks with a short title, a very long title, or one of your real posts.
5. Give it a name and click **Save** (or press Ctrl/⌘ + S).

Everything about the Studio is on the [Template Studio page](https://beautifulplugins.com/docs/image-generator/template-studio/).

> **Tip:** Template images are drawn on your own server — no API key, no cost, nothing sent anywhere. Build 3–5 templates and every post gets a branded image even if you never touch AI.

## Step 2 — Turn on automatic featured images

Go to **Image Generator → Settings → Automatic Featured Images**:

1. Tick **Enable Post Thumbnails** (and **Enable Page Thumbnails** if you want pages covered too).
2. Leave **Generate With** on **Image template**.
3. Under **Image Template**, pick the template you just made, or leave **A random template** to rotate through all your published templates.
4. Click **Save Changes**.

Now write a post, leave the featured image empty and click **Publish**. Reload the editor — the post has a featured image with its title on it, saved in your Media Library. Posts that already have a featured image are never touched. Details on the [Automatic Featured Images page](https://beautifulplugins.com/docs/image-generator/automatic-featured-images/).

## Step 3 — Try the block editor tools

Open any post in the block editor:

- In the document sidebar, open the **AI Featured Image** panel and click **Generate** to create the featured image with your automatic method, or **Choose a template, a stock photo or a prompt…** to open the image modal.
- Add an **Image** block and click the ✨ button in its toolbar to fill it from a template, a stock photo or an AI prompt.

See [Block Editor & Media Library](https://beautifulplugins.com/docs/image-generator/editor-integration/).

## Step 4 — Add stock photos (optional, free)

1. Get a free API key from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/oauth/applications), [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/api/new/) or [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/api/docs/) — you only need one.
2. Paste it under **Image Generator → Settings → Stock Photos** and click **Test Connection**.

A tab for that library appears in the image modal. You can also use stock photos for automatic featured images, or as the background of a template — see [Stock Photos](https://beautifulplugins.com/docs/image-generator/stock-photos/) and [Photo Templates](https://beautifulplugins.com/docs/image-generator/photo-templates/).

## Step 5 — Add AI images (optional)

1. Create an API key at [OpenAI](https://platform.openai.com/api-keys) and make sure billing is set up on your OpenAI account.
2. Paste it under **Image Generator → Settings → AI Service → API Key** and save.
3. In the editor, open the image modal's **Custom Prompt** tab, describe an image, and click **Generate**.

AI images are paid for with your key, so the plugin lets you decide who can use them and how many each user may create per hour. See [AI Images](https://beautifulplugins.com/docs/image-generator/ai-images/).
