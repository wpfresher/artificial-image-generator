# Image Generator Documentation

Welcome to the documentation for **Image Generator**, the AI featured image, image template and stock photo plugin for WordPress by [BeautifulPlugins](https://beautifulplugins.com/). Everything here applies to the free plugin from WordPress.org; pages covering the [Image Generator Pro](https://beautifulplugins.com/plugins/image-generator-pro/) add-on are marked as Pro.

New here? Start with [Installation](https://beautifulplugins.com/docs/image-generator/installation/) and then follow the [Getting Started guide](https://beautifulplugins.com/docs/image-generator/getting-started/) — it takes you from a fresh install to automatic featured images on every post in about ten minutes.

## Setup

- [Installation](https://beautifulplugins.com/docs/image-generator/installation/) — requirements, installing the free plugin and Pro, and updating.
- [Getting Started](https://beautifulplugins.com/docs/image-generator/getting-started/) — create your first template and switch on automatic featured images.
- [Settings](https://beautifulplugins.com/docs/image-generator/settings/) — every option explained: general, automatic featured images, AI service and stock photos.

## Image Templates

- [Image Templates](https://beautifulplugins.com/docs/image-generator/image-templates/) — the templates list: add, duplicate, set the default, export, and delete.
- [Template Studio](https://beautifulplugins.com/docs/image-generator/template-studio/) — the visual editor: canvas, layers, text, fonts, previews and keyboard shortcuts.
- [Merge Tags](https://beautifulplugins.com/docs/image-generator/merge-tags/) — `{title}`, `{category}`, `{custom_field:key}` and the rest, plus layers that show only when a tag has a value.
- [Photo Templates](https://beautifulplugins.com/docs/image-generator/photo-templates/) — a stock photo or AI image under your own design, different for every post.

## Generating Images

- [Automatic Featured Images](https://beautifulplugins.com/docs/image-generator/automatic-featured-images/) — a featured image for every post or page saved without one, from a template, AI or a stock photo.
- [Block Editor & Media Library](https://beautifulplugins.com/docs/image-generator/editor-integration/) — the ✨ block toolbar button, the AI Featured Image panel, and Generate Image in the Media Library.
- [AI Images](https://beautifulplugins.com/docs/image-generator/ai-images/) — OpenAI GPT Image: API key, models, prompts, styles, quality, variations and cost control.
- [Stock Photos](https://beautifulplugins.com/docs/image-generator/stock-photos/) — free photos from Unsplash, Pexels and Pixabay, with photographer credit.

## Pro
