# Pro Features

[Image Generator Pro](https://beautifulplugins.com/plugins/image-generator-pro/) is an add-on that extends the free plugin (v1.8.0+ required, both plugins active). It adds new building blocks to the [Template Studio](https://beautifulplugins.com/docs/image-generator/template-studio/) — everything in the free plugin stays free.

## Rating layer

Draw a rating on any template — for reviews, product roundups and recipes.

- **Value** — a number like `4.5`, or a tag such as `{rating}` or `{custom_field:rating}` so each post shows its own score.
- **Out of** — the scale, up to 10.
- **Shape** — Stars or Dots; values like 4.5 are drawn with a partly filled shape.
- **Align**, **Spacing**, **Color**, **Empty color** and **Empty opacity**.

## Progress bar layer

Show progress — "Part 3 of 8" in a course or series, a goal reached, a challenge day.

- **Value** and **Out of** — numbers, or tags such as `{custom_field:part}` and `{custom_field:parts}`.
- **Rounded ends**, **Color**, **Track color** and **Track opacity**.

## Badge layer

A shape with text that sizes itself to fit — for "NEW", "-30%", "Best pick" or "Free".

- **Shape** — Circle, Starburst, Pill or Rectangle.
- **Text** — plain text or tags like `{sale_percent}`, with its own **Font** and colors.

## WooCommerce tags

On WooCommerce products, four extra [merge tags](https://beautifulplugins.com/docs/image-generator/merge-tags/) put live product data on your images:

| Tag | Replaced with |
|---|---|
| `{price}` | The current price, in your store's currency format |
| `{regular_price}` | The regular price |
| `{sale_percent}` | The discount, e.g. "-33%" — empty when the product isn't on sale |
| `{rating}` | The average customer rating, e.g. "4.5" — empty when the product has no reviews |

Combine them with **Show only when**: show a sale badge only when `{sale_percent}` is not empty.

## Premium starter designs

Five more ready-made designs appear under **Start from a design**:

- **Product review with rating**
- **Sale announcement**
- **Course or series part**
- **Recipe card**
- **Product price tag**

## Templates never break

Pro layers and tags keep rendering even when your license expires or isn't active — templates that use them keep producing the same images. Only the Studio tools, the premium designs and updates need an active license. If you deactivate Pro, its layers are kept in your templates (shown as unavailable in the Studio) and come back when you reactivate it.
